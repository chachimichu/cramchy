I'm Charlene, a BS Psychology student at National University–Clark. I kept cramming for quizzes, exams, and deadlines, and wanted one app that could hold my school life together.

I couldn't find one that worked the way I needed, so I made one. It started as my first app and a little side quest; then I shared it with friends and other students.

the name has a snack story

Cram + chips
= Cramchy.

“Cram” came from how I was always cramming. “Chy” came from the chips I was eating when I thought of the name. Cramchy sounded a little like “crunchy,” and it stuck.
