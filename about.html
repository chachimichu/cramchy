the app

Built around real student routines.

Cramchy is an academic planning app created by student developer Charlene Mikaela D. Caco. It brings course schedules, deadlines, study blocks, exam planning, and grades together so students can see what needs attention and plan at their own pace.

Its academic term and exam tools are designed with National University students in mind, including separate Midterms and Finals workspaces. Cramchy is an independent student project and is not an official National University app.

the creator

Charlene Mikaela D. Caco

Charlene created Cramchy to make it easier to keep academic life in one place. She continues to shape the app around the everyday details students need: a clear weekly view, connected tasks, useful exam tools, and a gradebook that explains progress.
