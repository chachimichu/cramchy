why I built it

I needed all my school stuff in one place.

I was looking for something that could keep up with my actual school life: courses and schedules, tasks, study time, exams, and grades.

I kept finding apps that handled one part, but not everything I needed in the way I wanted to use it. So I started making my own.

Cramchy grew from there. When I needed something else for school, I tried to build it into the same app instead of adding another place to keep track of.
