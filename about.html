01why I made it

I needed one app
that could keep up
with all of school.

Courses. Class schedules. Tasks. Study time. Exams. Grades.

I kept looking for an app that could bring all of those together in a way that worked for me. I couldn’t find one, so I decided to make it myself.

Cramchy started as my side quest—and my first app. I built the tools I wanted to use, then shared it with friends and other students.

02and the name?

It’s Cramchy.
There’s a story.

“Cram” comes from how I used to cram for everything. “Chy” came from the chips I was eating when I thought of the name. Cramchy sounded a little like “crunchy,” and it stuck.

04who it’s for

For students.
Wherever you study.

You don’t have to be from NU to use Cramchy. Courses, schedules, tasks, and study tools are for any student who wants their school life together in one place.

The gradebook is the NU-specific part right now; it follows National University’s grading system. Cramchy is an independent student project, not an official NU app.
