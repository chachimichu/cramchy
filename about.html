01where it began

I wanted school
to feel a little
less scattered.

you know that feeling?

Honestly, I didn’t start with some huge master plan. I just wanted one place where I could see my classes, remember what was due, and figure out what to work on next.

Because keeping track of everything can become its own subject. The schedule is over here, the quiz reminder is somewhere else, and grades? A whole separate spiral.

So I started building the kind of study space I wished I already had: clear enough to help, gentle enough to come back to, and flexible when the week inevitably changes.
